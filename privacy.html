Privacy

Last updated October 18, 2026

ATLAS is a free school organizer made by one person. It has no server and no database of its own: your work stays with you, in your browser and, if you sign in, in your own Google Drive.

What ATLAS keeps, and where

Your classes, assignments, notes, flashcards, grades, focus history and settings are saved in the browser you use ATLAS in.

If you sign in with Google, a copy is also saved in a hidden ATLAS folder in your own Google Drive (Drive's “app data” folder), so your other devices can catch up. ATLAS can only see that one folder. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and nobody else, including the person who makes ATLAS, can read it.

What ATLAS gets from your Google account

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, used only to show who is signed in.
  • Access to ATLAS's own hidden folder in your Drive, used only to sync your desk.

That's all. ATLAS does not read your email, your calendar, your contacts or your files. ATLAS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI study tools

The AI buttons (summarize, make flashcards, quiz me and the rest) only run when you press one. When they do, the text you are working on is sent to Google:

  • If your computer has Chrome's on-device AI, it can run on the computer itself, and nothing leaves it.
  • Otherwise it goes to Google's Gemini service through ATLAS's Firebase project. Google's free tier is used, and Google may use what you send to improve its products. Please leave anything private out.

To stop other sites from using ATLAS's free AI, the AI tools are protected by reCAPTCHA; Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply. You can switch the AI tools off in Settings.

What ATLAS never does

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.
  • ATLAS loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.

Deleting your data

  • In ATLAS, open Settings, then Your data, then Erase everything.
  • To remove the Drive copy: in Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps, find ATLAS and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • To take back ATLAS's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/connections.

Students

ATLAS is made for students. If you are under the age where your country lets you agree to this on your own, use ATLAS with a parent's or guardian's permission. School-issued Google accounts may need the school's approval before they can sign in; ATLAS also works without an account.
